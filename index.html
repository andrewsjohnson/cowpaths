How the history becomes art

Each clinic keeps a separate timeline strand. A submission leaves its clinic at its month, then branches into pharmacy fulfillments, recipients, and medication counts. Stock deliveries use dashed paths.

Curvature, sparkle and depth are artistic choices. Point lights are texture, not extra orders. Months preserve chronology; exact dates and patient identities are not needed.

If the history exceeds this budget, a repeatable sample is shown and labeled. Counts below always distinguish drawn and available submissions.
